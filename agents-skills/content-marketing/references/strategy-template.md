# Content strategy doc template

The durable artifact of `content-marketing` (S12). One file per site/brand at
`docs/roles/marketing-strategist/strategy/<site>.md`. New docs fill all eight
sections in order; refreshes update the changed sections and bump the
"updated" date.

```markdown
# Content strategy — <site/brand>

- **Goal:** <the one goal this strategy serves — e.g. "grow organic traffic"
  / "drive qualified leads (the leads D1 table)">
- **Date:** <YYYY-MM-DD>
- **Updated:** <YYYY-MM-DD> (initial: <YYYY-MM-DD>)
- **Status:** active | needs-refresh
- **Method note:** <which SERP checks / sources grounded the intent and
  cluster sections — the source list or a pointer to a deep-research run>

## 1. Audience & intent

**Reader:** <who the reader is — one or two personas, concrete>

| Topic area | Intent bucket(s) | Target query | Three Cs (type / format / angle) |
|------------|------------------|--------------|----------------------------------|
| <pillar area> | informational / commercial / transactional / navigational | <the query> | <dominant type / format / angle from the SERP> |

Notes: multiple intents per query are normal (R7 Q3) — record the dominant
one and the caveat. "Follow the crowd": the three Cs come from the top-5
SERP, not from taste.

## 2. Positioning & guiding principles

- **Topical lane:** <what a first-time visitor immediately understands this
  site is about — the site-radius test>
- **Information-gain stance:** <the original data / firsthand experience /
  real effort this site brings that copycat content can't>
- **Brand-vs-information position:** <what makes this a brand, not an info
  blog>
- **Guiding principles:** <the 3–5 content principles the site stands on —
  the explicit answer to "what do we believe about the content we publish">

## 3. Content pillars

| Pillar | Seed topic | Pillar format (guide / what-is / how-to) | Why (demand + authority) |
|--------|-----------|------------------------------------------|--------------------------|
| <1–5 pillars> | | | |

Mutually exclusive, collectively covering the strategy. Each subtopic passed
the three filters (demand? relevance? topical focus?) — cuts are listed in
the exclusion list below.

## 4. Topic clusters

Per pillar: pillar → cluster → concrete article topics.

| Topic (working title) | Intent + three Cs | Rough demand | Subtopics to cover | Internal links (to pillar / to related clusters) |
|-----------------------|-------------------|--------------|--------------------|--------------------------------------------------|

- **Internal-link plan:** cluster ↔ pillar with the same hyperlinked keyword;
  cross-links between related clusters; no orphaned pages.
- **Exclusion list (the "do nothing" list):** <off-topic ideas that are
  interesting but off-lane — site focus is a hard constraint>

## 5. Funnel mapping

| Cluster / topic | Stage (TOFU / MOFU / BOFU) | Conversion action |
|-----------------|----------------------------|-------------------|

Pillar pages optimize for conversion; cluster pages optimize for traffic.
TOFU content must carry information gain (it's the surface AI Overviews
appear on). BOFU ends in <the site's conversion action — e.g. the lead form
for Bluebovine>.

## 6. Distribution

- **Owned channels:** <blog, YouTube, newsletter — what's real for this site>
- **Repurposing plan:** <how one cluster article becomes social / newsletter
  / video via content-writer formats>
- **Search-everywhere note:** <off-Google presence that feeds AI visibility>

## 7. Measurement

- **KPIs (cluster-level, not post-level):** <search visibility / organic
  conversions / non-branded traffic / (AI mentions, AI share of voice once
  tooling exists)>
- **ROI method (fixed up front):** <conversion analysis | lifetime traffic
  value | signup attribution — one, applied consistently>
- **Review cadence:** <e.g. quarterly, with the strategy review>
- **Refresh triggers (act immediately, don't wait for the review):** traffic
  drop from peak; superseded stat; changed tool; competitor substantially
  updated; dropped out of an AI Overview.

## 8. Content calendar (first 30/60/90 days)

Pillar first, then clusters. Cadence set against realistic output.

| Day window | Topic | Format | Funnel stage | Status |
|------------|-------|--------|--------------|--------|
| 1–30 | <pillar page> | <format> | <stage> | planned |
```

Field notes:

- **Goal** — one, SMART-ish, tied to the site's business purpose. The KPI set
  in section 7 must ladder up to it (R9: pick ~3 KPIs that ladder up, not a
  dashboard of everything).
- **Three Cs** (R7 Q3) — content *type* (blog post / video / product page /
  landing page), *format* (how-to / list / review / comparison), *angle* (the
  specific focus that makes top posts stand out — recency, a constraint, a
  sub-audience).
- **Rough demand** — a range with the method named (SERP volume, competitor
  coverage), never an invented number (S9 discipline).
- **Subtopics to cover** — from the top-ranking pages / content-gap check for
  the target query (R7 Q3: subtopic coverage = intent satisfaction).
- **Status** — planned → asked (board card exists) → drafted → QA → published
  (the CM-2 inventory row is the published record).
