## Competitive landscape

The market Bluebovine serves (chuck 2026-11-26: "product management
consulting") splits into six adjacent categories. Closest alternatives first.

### 1. Solo fractional-PM practitioners (closest alternative)

- **k8mak (Kate Makrigiannis)** — solo fractional PM / product coaching
  (k8mak.com). Positioning: senior practitioner selling fractional PM,
  fractional CPO, and coaching directly; content funnel ("Book a 20-min
  call"). Pricing signal (her own market write-up, updated 2026-10-17):
  IC-level fractional PM $100–200/hr or $4,000–8,000/mo; Head of Product
  $175–300/hr or $8,000–16,000/mo; VP/CPO $250–400/hr or $12,000–25,000/mo;
  project-based $10,000–50,000 (audit, strategy sprint, hiring plan);
  coaching $2,000–5,000/mo.
  Evidence: https://www.k8mak.com/blog/how-much-does-a-fractional-product-manager-cost/
  (fetched 2026-11-27).
- **Velonova** — fractional product leadership, one active client at a time,
  embedded. Positioning: "you pay for judgment and outcomes, not for a
  chair." Pricing (their own write-up, 2026-07-06): hourly/advisory
  $100–250/hr; light retainer $3,000–6,000/mo (~1 day/wk); embedded
  fractional PM $5,000–10,000/mo (2–3 days/wk); fractional CPO
  $7,000–13,000/mo; their own rate ₪12,000–20,000/mo.
  Evidence: https://velonova.io/blog/vol-17-fractional-product-manager/
  (fetched 2026-11-27).
- **Gap:** solo practitioners sell via their own blog/LinkedIn funnels and
  word of mouth; few have a *content-owned brand* (site + steady published
  analysis) that compounds trust and generates inbound — which is exactly
  what Bluebovine's slow-burn content strategy (Bluebovine O1, 3–4 posts/mo)
  is built to do. [inferred from the two positioning write-ups above]

### 2. Marketplaces & directories (fractional/coaching)

- **Leland** (joinleland.com/product-management) — marketplace of 1:1 PM
  coaches. Observed 2026-11-27: PM coaches listed at $59–379/hr; the visible
  roster is overwhelmingly **career coaching** (break into PM, interview
  prep, "23 offers landed"), not company-side product consulting.
  Evidence: https://www.joinleland.com/product-management (fetched 2026-11-27).
- **MentorCruise** — 606 vetted product coaches, 1-on-1; "ranges from
  $69/hour for one-off sessions to $25,000/quarter for enterprise."
  Evidence: https://mentorcruise.com/coach/product/ (search snippet,
  2026; page not fetched).
- **arc.dev** — freelance product-coach directory ("Best Freelance Product
  Coaches for Hire in Nov 2026"). Evidence:
  https://arc.dev/hire-product-managers/product-coaches (search snippet,
  2026-11).
- **Toptal** — vetted freelance marketplace including product managers;
  pricing not verified (Cloudflare-blocked from this environment).
  Evidence: https://www.toptal.com/product-managers (blocked 2026-11-27).
- **Gap:** marketplaces commoditize *career* coaching; the company-side
  consulting end (roadmaps, org design, AI product strategy) is thin there —
  buyers of a $5k+/mo embedded PM don't shop Leland. [inferred, grounded in
  the Leland roster observed 2026-11-27]

### 3. PM education with consulting-adjacent arms

- **Reforge** — the scale player: cohort courses + community. Pricing
  (reforge.com/pricing, fetched 2026-11-27): Individual 1 seat **$1,995/yr**;
  Starter 10 seats **$9,995/yr**; Scale 30 seats **$23,995/yr**; 40+ live
  courses, 600+ on-demand, 1,400+ guides. Sells *knowledge*, not execution —
  the natural "why not just take a course?" competitor.
  Evidence: https://www.reforge.com/pricing (fetched 2026-11-27).
- **Product School** — certificates/courses; pricing not verified (site
  redirected/blocked from this environment). Evidence:
  https://www.product.school/pricing (unfetched 2026-11-27).
- **Gap:** education answers "how do I do product?" but not "fix *our*
  product" — the consulting buyer's actual question. Education is a
  complement (and a lead source), not a substitute. [inferred]

### 4. Communities (adjacent)

- **MindTheProduct** — community/events; publishes the reference explainer on
  fractional PM ("a growing leadership model in which an experienced product
  leader works with a company on a part-time basis", 2024-09-16).
  Evidence: https://www.mindtheproduct.com/what-is-fractional-product-management/
- **Lenny's Newsletter** — the category's data/newsletter hub; its PM-jobs
  tracking (TrueUp data) is the demand signal this analysis uses.
  Evidence: https://www.lennysnewsletter.com (referenced via UXCam roundup
  below, 2026-11-06).
- **Gap:** communities monetize attention, not outcomes; they validate the
  category's growth but don't sell embedded work. [inferred]

  https://www.mckinsey.com/featured-insights/mckinsey-on-books/rewired

### 6. PM tools with consulting arms

- Jira/Aha!/Productboard et al. sell software; consulting is delivered by
  their partner ecosystems (e.g., Atlassian consulting partners for Jira).
  No specific pricing gathered. [inferred / unverified — see "What I did not
  verify"]. Tooling partners sell *implementation of the tool*, not product
  strategy — different job-to-be-done.

### Do nothing (mandatory column)

The founder-as-PM / "we'll figure it out" default. Evidence it's a real
competitor: k8mak's own write-up frames the market around teams where "a
founder is acting as PM" and teams that "burn $200K in engineering time
building the wrong thing because nobody asked 'why are we building this?'"
(k8mak.com, 2026-10-17); Product Focus 2026: 60% of product people are pulled
into unplanned work and only 25% spend most of their time on strategy (via
UXCam roundup, 2026-11-06). Inaction is free, so every paid alternative must
price against the cost of inaction (wrong builds, stalled roadmaps), not
just against each other.

**Why them (top alternative, solo fractional PM):** they are already selling
exactly the job Bluebovine wants, at proven prices, with practitioner
credibility and an existing client base. A solo practitioner with 10 years of
visible product work wins on trust today.

**Why us:** Bluebovine is the only player in this scan pairing the consulting
offer with a *content-owned, compounding brand* aimed at the same buyer —
the slow-burn site (Bluebovine O1) is the distribution asset none of the
solo practitioners have, and the org runs it as a system (strategy doc,
calendar, QA gate) rather than a blog. The marketplace/education players own
the low end (career coaching) and the knowledge end; the middle — embedded
product judgment for funded-but-lean product teams, discovered via owned
content — is the open lane. [inferred, grounded in the matrix above]

## Market signals

- **PM hiring recovered to 2022 highs** — 7,300+ open PM roles at tech
  companies worldwide in March 2026, +20% in Q1 2026, 75% above the early-2023
  low (Lenny's Newsletter / TrueUp data). Source:
  https://uxcam.com/blog/product-management-statistics/ (2026-11-06, fetched
  2026-11-27).
- **AI product gap** — 70% of product leaders increased AI budgets but only
  6% of teams have set explicit AI OKRs; 26% of PMs fear AI could replace
  them and only 39% have had comprehensive job-specific AI training; 688 open
  AI-PM roles (May 2025). Source: same UXCam roundup (2026-11-06).
- **Product teams are firefighting, not strategizing** — 60% of product people
  pulled into unplanned work; only 25% spend most of their time on strategy;
  71% say they don't spend enough time with customers (75% among heads/
  directors) (Product Focus, 2026). Source: same UXCam roundup.
- **Fractional PM is an established, priced category** — two independent 2026
  rate write-ups (k8mak 2026-10-17; Velonova 2026-07-06) agree on
  $100–350/hr and $3–25k/mo tiers; MindTheProduct (2024-09-16) calls
  fractional PM "a growing leadership model."
- **Coaching market is career-coaching-heavy** — Leland's visible PM roster
  (fetched 2026-11-27) is dominated by break-into-PM/interview coaching at
  $59–379/hr; company-side consulting is scarce on marketplaces.
  [inferred from the observed roster]
- **PM pay keeps rising** — US median PM total pay $233,000 (base $187,000),
  +4.55% in 2025 vs +2.67% for SWEs (Levels.fyi Eoy Pay Report 2025, via UXCam
  roundup). Rising salary = rising price of the full-time alternative, which
  widens the fractional value gap.

## Market size & share

Per the PM-1 method: **bottom-up only**; every figure labeled
measured / proxy / estimate; ranges with explicit assumptions — no point
estimates, no invented figures.

**Demand anchor (measured):** 7,300+ open PM roles at tech companies
worldwide, March 2026 (Lenny's Newsletter/TrueUp via UXCam, 2026-11-06).
Each open role is a company with a live product-leadership gap it is trying
to fill — with a $233k/yr US median hire (Levels.fyi, measured) or,
increasingly, with fractional help.

**TAM (proxy):** companies worldwide with a live product-leadership gap that
could be filled by external product help at fractional pricing.
- Equation: open PM roles (proxy for gap-having companies) × annual
  fractional spend.
- 7,300 roles × $60k–240k/yr (k8mak's fractional annual range, 2026-10-17)
  = **~$0.4B–1.8B/yr**.
- Assumptions: (a) one open role ≈ one company gap [proxy — flow, not stock;
  ignores companies with unfilled gaps that aren't posting]; (b) the
  fractional annual range applies to the gap's alternative spend [estimate];
  (c) worldwide scope, USD.
- Label: **proxy** (demand-flow anchored, not a market census).

**SAM (estimate):** the segment Bluebovine can serve — US, remote-friendly,
seed→Series-B / mid-market product teams (the buyer who won't go to McKinsey
and won't shop Leland).
- Equation: US share of open roles × SAM-relevant roles × entry- to
  mid-tier annual spend.
- US share of open roles: assume 40–60% [estimate — no 2026 source found;
  Bay Area alone is 23% of open roles per Lenny's via UXCam].
- 3,000–4,400 US roles × $60k–100k/yr (coaching/light-retainer/embedded-PM
  tiers, k8mak 2026-10-17) = **~$0.2B–0.4B/yr**.
- Label: **estimate** (two stacked assumptions on top of the measured anchor).

**SOM (estimate):** what a one-operator practice with a content-led funnel
can take in year 1–2.
- Equation: clients × retainer. Capacity: 1–3 embedded clients (Velonova
  runs exactly one at a time) at $5k–10k/mo, plus 1–2 project engagements
  ($10k–50k) per year.
- Year 1: 1–2 clients × $60k–120k/yr + 1–2 projects × $10k–50k =
  **~$70k–290k/yr**. Year 2 (content compounding): 2–3 clients + 2–3
  projects = **~$150k–550k/yr**.
- Assumptions: content funnel converts at an unknown rate [estimate — no
  Bluebovine traffic data yet; Plausible wiring (G3) pending]; capacity
  ceiling is the operator's time, not the market.
- Label: **estimate** (capacity-bound, not market-bound).

**Share:** Bluebovine's current market share is **0 — measured** (no revenue;
org phase "define", `docs/org/goals.md` cycle 2026-12). The analysis question
is therefore not share capture against incumbents but *which slice of the
open middle to enter first* — answered by the opportunity list below.

**Cross-check (sanity, not a sizing input):** the broader US "product
management consulting" directory count is in the thousands of firms
(RevenueBase: 4,693 matching firms in the US, 2026-11-04,
https://revenuebase.ai/companies/product-management-consulting-firms/united-states
— top-25 list is client-rendered; names not extracted). A multi-thousand-firm
directory against a ~$0.2–0.4B SAM implies a long tail of tiny players —
consistent with the "open middle" read. [inferred]

## Opportunities

Each opportunity: gap/signal → size (bottom-up range) → assumptions →
evidence. (1) and (2) are flagged **decision-ready for the O1 KR2 exec
pitch** (2026-12-22 due).

### Opportunity 1 — Embedded fractional product leadership for seed→Series-B
(decision-ready for KR2)

- **Gap/signal:** teams with a live product-leadership gap (7,300+ open
  roles, 2026-03) can't or won't pay a $233k/yr full-time hire; solo
  practitioners serve this at $5–10k/mo but are found via word of mouth /
  personal funnels, not a content-owned brand.
- **Size (bottom-up, range):** 1–3 clients × $5k–10k/mo = **$60k–360k/yr**
  (SOM-bound, not market-bound).
- **Assumptions:** 1–3 concurrent clients (operator capacity); $5–10k/mo is
  the embedded-PM tier (k8mak 2026-10-17, Velonova 2026-07-06); content
  funnel drives at least some inbound [estimate].
- **Evidence:** k8mak rate table
  (https://www.k8mak.com/blog/how-much-does-a-fractional-product-manager-cost/);
  Velonova tier table (https://velonova.io/blog/vol-17-fractional-product-manager/);
  demand anchor (https://uxcam.com/blog/product-management-statistics/).
- **Fastest test:** one public "fractional PM for seed/Series-B" positioning
  post + a 20-min intake call; measure: 5 qualified calls in 6 weeks.

### Opportunity 2 — AI product strategy advisory ("AI OKR sprint")
(decision-ready for KR2)

- **Gap/signal:** 70% of product leaders increased AI budgets but only 6% of
  teams have explicit AI OKRs; 26% of PMs fear AI displacement, 39% trained;
  688 open AI-PM roles (2025-05). Budgets are moving without strategy — a
  classic advisory window.
- **Size (bottom-up, range):** 2–6 engagements/yr × $10k–50k fixed-scope
  (k8mak project tier) = **$20k–300k/yr**, and a natural upsell path into
  Opportunity 1 retainers.
- **Assumptions:** buyers are the same seed→Series-B + mid-market teams as
  Opportunity 1; fixed-scope sprint format is deliverable by one operator in
  2–4 weeks [estimate].
- **Evidence:** UXCam AI stats (https://uxcam.com/blog/product-management-statistics/,
  2026-11-06); project-based pricing tier (k8mak, 2026-10-17).
- **Fastest test:** publish one "AI OKR gap" analysis post (fits the
  slow-burn content calendar); measure: 3 inbound "can you do this for us"
  replies in 6 weeks.

### Opportunity 3 — Productized product audit / strategy sprint as the entry
  offer

- **Gap/signal:** consulting entry is fuzzy ("book a call, scope ad hoc");
  k8mak documents a $10k–50k project tier (audit, strategy sprint, hiring
  plan) that no one in the scan has productized with a public scope/price.
  A fixed, published offer de-risks the first buy and feeds both
  Opportunities 1 and 2.
- **Size (bottom-up, range):** 1–2 audits/quarter × $10k–50k =
  **$40k–400k/yr** at maturity; year-1 realistic 1–2 = **$10k–50k**.
- **Assumptions:** fixed scope (e.g., 2-week audit → roadmap + 90-day plan);
  price at the low-mid of the documented tier to win the first 3 logos
  [estimate].
- **Evidence:** k8mak project tier (https://www.k8mak.com/blog/how-much-does-a-fractional-product-manager-cost/);
  the "why them / why us" note above (no productized offer observed in the
  scan).
- **Fastest test:** publish the audit scope + price on the site; measure:
  first paid audit within 8 weeks.

### Opportunity 4 — Content-owned inbound as the structural moat (GTM, not
  an offer)

- **Gap/signal:** every alternative in the scan rents its distribution
  (marketplaces, personal social, community platforms); Reforge proves
  content+community scale but at an education price point. Bluebovine's
  existing slow-burn content system (3–4 posts/mo, QA-gated, Bluebovine O1)
  is the only consulting-side asset that compounds without marginal cost.
- **Size (bottom-up, range):** not sized directly — it multiplies
  Opportunities 1–3 by reducing customer-acquisition cost to ~0 (organic);
  the measurable target is inbound volume, not revenue [estimate].
- **Assumptions:** 12 months of consistent publishing before compounding
  effects are measurable [estimate]; Plausible wiring (G3) needed to measure.
- **Evidence:** the feature matrix above (content-owned channel row);
  Reforge's content-led scale (https://www.reforge.com/pricing); k8mak's own
  funnel pattern (blog → "book a 20-min call").
- **Fastest test:** the first 3 posts of the content calendar aimed at the
  fractional-PM buyer (vs. general PM audience); measure: search + direct
  inbound on the site.
